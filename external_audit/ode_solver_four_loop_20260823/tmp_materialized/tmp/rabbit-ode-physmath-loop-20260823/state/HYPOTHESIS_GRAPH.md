# HYPOTHESIS_GRAPH

Phase 4 candidate space, constructed only from the sanitized Phase-3 premises. All entries are active hypotheses, not implementation authority or validation claims.

## H0 - null / unmeasured-creep hypothesis

CORE_CLAIM: No current physics reformulation is yet justified as a D-071 remedy because the retained run did not identify the creep mechanism; the only warranted near-term work is correctness repair plus zero/new-production-code discriminators.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: D-071/V2 remain the current measurement authority; absence of a measured mechanism is not treated as proof that no mechanism exists.

MECHANISM: The apparent wall may be dominated by per-RHS collision cost, rejected-step dynamics, ill-conditioned derivatives, grid/domain effects, or an unrecorded solver state; selecting one physics reduction before separating these is underdetermined.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: Explains why generic solver/Jacobian labels and the current R5 narrative cannot inherit endpoint authority. It does not repair false-success defects.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Conservative null that can delay a genuine method if discriminators are poorly chosen.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: Prospective derivative, spectrum, tail, and clock discriminators will fail to show a large, grid-stable, endpoint-relevant margin, or will localize the cause to a different family.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: H5 conditional AP reduction.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: On frozen physical states spanning early/activation/creep/cold regions, record exact per-block RHS/J/Jv costs, accepted/rejected-attempt context, admissibility margins, projected collision spectrum, and observable-weighted tail residuals without claiming a trajectory.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: A prospectively sealed candidate shows a robust mechanism, survives the relevant invariants, and projects the full registered prefix/endpoint inside the unchanged budget with margin.

STATUS: active-null

## H1 - two-layer physical-domain admission

CORE_CLAIM: A two-layer contract combining strict physical coordinates/raw invariant checks with typed software terminal-cause precedence can eliminate the known false-physical-success family without clipping or post-failure observable consumption.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Coordinate decode remains representable in the visited regime; each physical residual is exact or controlled for its channel; last accepted and failing raw states are preserved separately.

MECHANISM: Logit/log representations encode open/positive domains, while a separate solver certificate admits output only after success, expected terminal cause, finite residual/bracket, physical domain, balance, normalization, and complete counters.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: C4-C7/H2-H6/H11/M1-M2-style correctness defects. No D-071 speed or endpoint claim.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Coordinates can saturate; physics does not determine event/status taxonomy; not every Python caller shares the Rust state layout.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: Known missed-event, contradictory-status, nonfinite, clipped-negative, and post-failure-observable mutants are all rejected while accepted raw cases and exact limits are unchanged.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: Software-only typed outcome repair with unchanged physical coordinates.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: A table-driven mutant suite over initial/exact zero events, wrong direction, nonfinite probe, refinement exhaustion, invalid raw occupation/H/abundance, and failed partial state; compare H1 to software-only repair.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: H1 hides a raw invalid state, rejects a valid strict-domain case due to coordinate saturation, or adds no discrimination beyond software-only precedence.

STATUS: active-correctness

## H2 - conditional energy coordinate and thermodynamic clock

CORE_CLAIM: On a certified flat-FLRW collision-coupled branch, evolving energy variables and optionally using `x=-ln(T_gamma/T_ref)` can reduce thermodynamic cancellation/derivative conditioning and replace the physical cold endpoint root by a fixed terminal coordinate.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Finite positive EOS derivative; single-valued local EOS inversion; `alpha=-d ln(T_gamma)/dN` has a prospectively sealed positive lower bound; no silent extension to nonzero shear.

MECHANISM: With `Q>0` into neutrinos, use `d rho_nu/dN=-4 rho_nu+Q/H` and `d rho_em/dN=-3(rho_em+P_em)-Q/H`; recover temperature from the EOS. If `alpha>0`, then `dN/dx=1/alpha` and the endpoint is fixed in `x`.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: Thermodynamic finite-difference conditioning, exchange cancellation, and the specific temperature endpoint event. It does not repair general event semantics or reduce collision-kernel cost by itself.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Missing full-path transversality/inversion certificate; Type-I shear work is not derived; division by small `alpha` can worsen conditioning.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: A scale ladder shows more stable thermodynamic derivatives/residuals than raw temperature coordinates, the EOS round-trip is bounded, and `alpha` remains above the frozen margin on all admitted states.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: Current temperature-in-N evolution with analytic thermodynamic derivatives and a certified event refiner.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: Use the same frozen physical-state set to test EOS round-trip, first-law residual, derivative-condition ladder, and `alpha` margin; kill before any trajectory if one state violates the certificate.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Nonunique/ill-conditioned inversion, nonpositive `alpha` margin, first-law/sign mismatch, or no derivative-conditioning benefit against the competitor.

STATUS: active-conditional

## H3 - channel-derived affinity and structured derivative slice

CORE_CLAIM: Deriving the exact current-channel detailed-balance form and left nullspaces, then exposing physically scaled analytic/block derivatives on the endpoint-consumed Rust path, can remove invalid Jv evidence and may yield measurable local activation/cold-path savings.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Exact gain/loss legs and chemical-potential conventions are derived from current bytes; only valid channel/bath invariants are imposed; state and independent-N directions are scaled separately; passive elapsed time is excluded from nonlinear geometry.

MECHANISM: Rewrite near-cancelling gain-loss algebra through a derived affinity `A=log(G/L)` and a cancellation-safe evaluation, then apply analytic/block Jv and measured sparse/Schur/Krylov structure only if the actual N48 pattern warrants it.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: Invalid physical-prefix JVP, missing production derivative authority, local collision derivative cost and detailed-balance cancellation. It is not by itself a D-071 remedy.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Current affinity/nullspaces/full Jv are unestablished; the source speed factor is not transferable; V2 bounds Jacobian-only leverage far below endpoint need.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: Common-equilibrium flux is numerically null with correct linearization; valid invariant rows annihilate the collision action/Jacobian; centered scale ladders agree with an independent high-precision small-grid reference; same-case full-RHS-equivalent calls or wall improve at least 10% without hidden dense fallback.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: Existing analytic dense blocks plus carefully scaled centered finite differences, without affinity or matrix-free restructuring.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: Derive and test one representative channel's exact gain/loss identity and invariant rows, then run a frozen-state Jv epsilon ladder and cost accounting; do not build a new solver backend.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Wrong channel identity/nullspace, non-grid-stable Jv, invariant or raw-parity failure, hidden dense work, or less than 10% same-case movement.

STATUS: active-local-performance

## H4 - observable-aware tail and discretization certificate

CORE_CLAIM: A rigorous extended-domain comparison plus propagated error enclosure can replace the current population/moment-only tail argument and decide whether any reduced domain/quadrature is safe for coupled BBN observables.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: The enclosure covers actual finite-mass collision kernels, blocking and routed momenta; direct source error and propagated feedback are separated; an independent reference is available.

MECHANISM: Bound lost collision action and its effect on energy transfer and weak-rate functionals, then propagate those intervals to `Yp`, `D/H`, and `Neff`; choose a grid/domain rule only when all intervals close prospectively.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: Tier-2 grid drift and absence of reaction-tail authority. It can certify or kill a surrogate but does not guarantee runtime reduction.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Current positive quadrature/moment tests supply no such theorem; sensitivity propagation may be too loose or costly; energy-only agreement is insufficient.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: A frozen grid/domain ladder gives containing, contracting error intervals for collision action, energy, both weak rates, and final observables, including the extended-domain oracle.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: Direct adaptive/full-domain computation using the temporary number-of-record without a formal enclosure.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: On a tiny representative channel/state set, test whether an analytic/numerical tail enclosure actually contains an independently extended-domain lost-action oracle and whether weak-rate weighting changes the selected domain.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Oracle escapes the bound, intervals do not contract, weak-rate and energy criteria select incompatible domains without a joint bound, or propagated intervals exceed frozen hard caps.

STATUS: active-certificate

## H5 - conditional collision-invariant micro-macro/AP reduction

CORE_CLAIM: Only if the current discrete collision operator has exact channel/bath invariants and a grid-stable projected fast/slow separation can a micro-macro/AP vertical slice materially change the D-071 asymptotic wall while preserving BBN observables.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Exact conserved macro rows and equilibrium manifold; measured non-null spectrum with usable gap across a frozen regime; controlled initial layer; overlap/handoff; H4 response/tail certificate; Rust endpoint-consumed implementation.

MECHANISM: Decompose the distribution into an equilibrium macro manifold plus a constrained micro deviation, evolve the fast collision subspace implicitly or by a validated penalization, retain full spectral dynamics through the non-separated window, and enter collisionless evolution only after integrated source bounds close.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: The orders-of-magnitude endpoint wall if a real scale separation exists. It is intentionally distinct from the rejected fixed momentum-cut algebraic slaving.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Generic AP literature is only a checklist; no current `Q`, spectrum, handoff, or response certificate exists; V2 found no usable fixed-cut gap.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: Projected spectra show a grid-stable gap on a prospectively frozen regime; full/AP overlap closes all invariants and H4 observables; the sealed N=0.14/0.22 to N>=0.25 discriminator stays within 5500 full-RHS-equivalent calls and 64800 s and yields an endpoint projection below budget with uncertainty margin.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: H0 plus full spectral dynamics with H3 local derivative improvements.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: Zero/new-production-code derivation of exact current channel invariants and projected non-null spectra across the stalled band; stop immediately if the gap is absent or grid-unstable.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: No exact invariant/equilibrium structure, no usable gap, non-well-prepared layer, failed overlap/first-law/positivity/observable bounds, or sealed prefix/budget miss.

STATUS: active-high-risk

## H6 - independent physics-mutant falsifier architecture

CORE_CLAIM: A small set of independently derived limiting cases and deliberately injected counterexamples can detect common-mode false greens that backend parity and ordinary happy-path tests miss.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Each limit is exact or controlled in the implemented model; the independent expected result does not reuse the same solver/tableau; mutants represent actual retained defect classes.

MECHANISM: Combine collisionless `df(q)/dN=0`, common-equilibrium collision null, finite-mass first law, reaction stoichiometry/baryon-charge, FLRW/LRS limits, and transverse/grazing/wrong-direction event cases with mutant killing.

EXPLANATION_RANGE: Validation false greens and missing negative-path coverage. It does not certify a full trajectory or solve the runtime wall.

KNOWN_LIMITS: Current ingredients are static; mutant efficacy is unexecuted; some constraints are invalid outside their channel or shear limit.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTION: Each known mutant is killed by at least one independently computed oracle while unmodified bounded good paths remain unchanged.

STRONGEST_COMPETITOR: Existing cross-backend parity and broad green suites without mutant injection.

CHEAPEST_DISCRIMINATOR: Implement no production change; run one table of known counterexamples against independent analytic/reduced-limit expectations and report mutation score by defect class.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Expected limits share the defective code path, invariants are asserted outside scope, or known mutants survive.

STATUS: active-validation
