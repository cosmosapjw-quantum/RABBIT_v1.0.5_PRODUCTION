## 2. The datum V1 could not supply: `num_jac`'s step factor runs away without bound

SciPy's `num_jac` keeps a **stateful per-component step factor** and multiplies it by ten whenever a
difference is judged too small. Because the factor persists on the solver across refreshes, the
escalation compounds. Logged at every refresh:

```text
refresh   at_raw        N     max(factor)/sqrt(EPS)   >10x   >100x   >1e4x
   0        521    0.16282                  1e+04      51      35       0
   1        712    0.16282                  1e+05      51      36       2
   4       1317    0.16287                  1e+06      51      36       3
   8       2110    0.16307                  1e+10      51      36       3
  12       2889    0.16323                  1e+14      51      36       3
  17       3866    0.16349                  1e+19      51      36       3
```

From refresh 4 onward the maximum climbs **exactly one decade per Jacobian refresh, monotonically,
with no ceiling**. At the last refresh the factor is `1e19 × sqrt(EPS) ≈ 1.5e11`, so those columns
are differenced over a step of order `1e11` in a state variable of magnitude ~30. **51 components
have ratcheted above 10×, 36 above 100×.**

The cause is structural, not incidental: the ratcheting components are in the momentum tail, where
occupations are ~1e-13 and the right-hand side is a near-cancellation of gain and loss. Their
difference quotients are always judged "too small", so the factor is raised at every refresh,
forever.

## 3. The chain, with each link's evidence

1. **The factor runs away** — §2, measured, unambiguous.
2. **The resulting Jacobian columns are wrong.** *This is the link V2 failed to measure* (§4). It is
   independently established at a *lower* ratchet level by the V1C recomputation: at factor
   `1e5 × sqrt(EPS)` the stored tail diagonals were wrong by up to **59.2%**, with 14 components over
   1%. At `1e19` no meaningful derivative survives.
3. **Newton fails, and refreshing does not help.** 69 Newton non-convergences, of which **50 occur
   with `jac_was_current = true`** — a freshly built Jacobian. That is expected if the refresh
   rebuilds the same corrupted matrix with the same ratcheted factor.
4. **The order cannot recover.** SciPy's BDF only reconsiders the order once `n_equal_steps >=
   order + 1`, and every Newton failure resets `n_equal_steps` to zero. With failures arriving
   continuously the order is pinned. **Measured median order in the creep: 1** — implicit Euler.
5. **Progress collapses.** At order 1 with `h` ≈ 1.1e-5, the run advances 1.94e-07 in N per raw call
   and projects to 5.6 years.

**Accuracy is never the binding constraint anywhere in this.** Across the whole run there were
**92 error tests and zero failures**, with a maximum error norm of 0.131 against a rejection
threshold of 1. The creep is entirely Newton-limited.

## 4. The instrument defect, stated plainly

The checkpoint Jacobians — the observation that would have measured link 2 directly — **all failed**:

```text
[04:08:18] checkpoint 1000: FAILED (preserved) -- CapReached: call_cap:4000
[04:08:22] checkpoint 2000: FAILED (preserved) -- CapReached: call_cap:4000
[04:08:26] checkpoint 3000: FAILED (preserved) -- CapReached: call_cap:4000
```

The observation-only Jacobians were computed through the same right-hand-side wrapper that enforces
the integration call cap, and were not exempted from it. V1 had a `budget_mode` split for exactly
this and V2 dropped it. The saved accepted states were held in memory and were lost with the process,
so the measurement cannot be recovered without re-running.

**This is the second consecutive run in which an implementation defect cost a sealed conjunct**, and
the pattern is now worth naming: the sealed *criteria* are now mechanically bound to the analyser
(§5), but the sealed *observations* are not bound to anything. A protocol that names an observation
should fail loudly at startup if the code cannot produce it, not silently at the end.

## 5. What the governance fix did and did not do

The V2 analyser loads its criteria from the sealed protocol by digest and refuses to run on drift.

## 7. What this means, stated at the strength the evidence supports

**Proposed, with strong measured support:** the ~5-year projection is very likely an artifact of a
runaway in SciPy's finite-difference step heuristic interacting with sub-tolerance momentum-tail
occupations — not a property of the physics, and not even a property of the discretisation. Four of
the five links are directly measured; the second is measured only at a lower ratchet level, in a
different run.

**Not concluded, and important:** this does not reopen anything. Under D-071 and V0 §7, repairing a
diagnosed tolerance or coordinate pathology — pinning the finite-difference step, capping the factor,
supplying an analytic Jacobian — is instrument tuning and is disqualified. The gate stays FAIL on its
preserved measurement. What has changed is the *reading* of that measurement: the instrument's
failure now has a named, measured, mundane candidate cause, and the underlying physical question is
untouched by it.

which is 2% of the trajectory.
