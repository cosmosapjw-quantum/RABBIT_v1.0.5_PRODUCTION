# V3 protocol — exhaustive tracking of every unmeasured item, sealed before the instrument exists

**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Authority:** owner grant — track every still-unmeasured item without exception and issue a
verified report; overnight runs, workflows, and subagents authorised. Parallel execution falls under
the standing parallel-run grant: every axis is reported, no post-hoc selection.
**Sealed before any V3 output byte.** Nothing in this document may be edited after the first run
byte; a wrong prediction or a miscalibrated threshold is preserved as such.

**Claim ceiling, identical to V0/V2:** diagnostic evidence only. Nothing here can satisfy any D-071
reopen conjunct, move a gate, or license a repair — pinning the finite-difference step, capping the
ratchet factor, and supplying an analytic Jacobian remain disqualified as instrument tuning
(D-071, V0 §7). `implies_status: null`. The board stays **6 PASS / 2 FAIL**.

**The governance addition this protocol introduces.** V1 lost a sealed conjunct to re-typed
criteria; V2 lost one to an unproducible observation. The criteria fix (digest-loaded, two-way
cover) carries over from V2. New here: an **observation manifest** — every observation this protocol
names must be *proved producible before integration begins*, by a startup self-test that exercises
the full observation path (including its budget exemption) on a miniature problem and verifies every
named artifact appears. A leg whose self-test fails refuses to start: `FAIL_PROTOCOL` at minute
zero, not at hour six.

## 1. The ledger — every unmeasured item, tracked to closure or to a named gap

Status vocabulary (sealed): `CLOSED` — every closure condition evaluated true. `PARTIAL` — at least
one true and at least one false or unevaluable. `UNMEASURED` — none true, reasons recorded. Closure
conditions are **measurement-existence** predicates: an item is CLOSED when its question is
*answered*, whichever way the answer goes. The answer itself is evidence payload, never a closure
condition — measuring is not confirming.

## 5. Failure preservation

Line-buffered sinks from the first byte; state vectors written to disk at the moment of capture
(V2's checkpoint states died with the process); output roots are never deleted or reused, so every
`FAIL_PROTOCOL` payload survives; every exception, cap breach and non-reproduction is retained
verbatim and reported. Wall attribution across concurrent legs is excluded from claims (contention),
as in V0 §5; step, Newton, factor and spectrum data are unaffected by concurrency.
